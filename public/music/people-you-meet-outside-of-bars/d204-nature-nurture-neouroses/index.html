d204.07 : nature nurture neuroses

the seventh roll of d204, a project where i let dice tell me which of my songs to record & a vlog abt revisiting the artistic viciousness of a teenager who is Going Thru It & healing from difficult parents so you don’t become them.

  • Jul 15, 2024
  • in:
  • people you meet outside of bars
  • d204
  • video
  • original

d204 is a project where i take a list of eighty songs that i wrote & record whichever one comes up when i roll a d20 & a d4

the song

my lover has left me & she’s just like her father
snorting cocaine off of someone else’s daughter

the vlog

Ultimately, a lot of my healing has been a reflection of what my parents were not able to do– I think as is probably often true. I don’t want to have the effect on the world that they did. I want to be able to understand why they acted the way they did and I want to get myself to a place where I feel, more than they did, that I have a choice to act differently. And it’s interesting having that come up in a song that I wrote so young.
