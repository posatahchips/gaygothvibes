finding out that one of the songs from my “dead estranged mother” EP ended up on a harrowhark nonagesimus playlist just made my entire week

this is not an album whose pieces i ever expect to resonate out of context & this specific switching of context made me, like… SO so happy. it’s amazing. thank you to the person who included this incredibly deep cut on their locked tomb fanmix. it makes me feel seen in a sort of giddy absurd way to know my music can be used to soundtrack fictional lesbians with brain problems
